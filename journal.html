Why Guided SOAR

The Bible is a book about God. Guided SOAR is a simple way to read it so that, passage by passage, you see Him more clearly and let that change you.

Scripture
Step back before you dive in.

Knowing who wrote a book, when, and why gives you perspective to read it the way it was meant to be read, not just the way it sounds to us today.

Observation
Slow down.

Notice words, wrestle with hard ideas, follow connections. It’s okay not to have every answer today. Understanding grows a little at a time.

Central Truth
Keep the main thing the main thing.

We read to know God, not just to find tips for life. That’s why every page has His character at the center, because the knowledge of God and the knowledge of self go hand in hand.

Application
Understand first, then apply.

Ask what it says, then what it means, and only then what it means for you. Application built on the text will hold up.

Reflection
Pray the whole way through.

Before, during, and after. The same Spirit who inspired these words helps us understand them and live them out.
